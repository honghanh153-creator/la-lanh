# Tarot pass 2: position text in plain Vietnamese (29/10/2026)

**Status: implemented on branch `feat/tarot-position-plain` (on top of PR #115, head `676a71c`) for exact-SHA
review; not merged, not released. Owner reading pending before release** (fallback approval rule): the 177 card
sentences in `docs/reviews/2026-10-29-tarot-pass2-card-sentences.xlsx`, the frame table below and the samples.

## Why

Owner feedback in chat, 29/10/2026: "content tarot vẫn kỳ lắm". PR #115 made the summary and headline plain; the
position blocks still read oddly:

| Problem | Example (renderer v9 at `676a71c`) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The frame is "label: card text", and the label repeats the position header. | Under "Điều đã có dữ kiện": "Nhìn chung, bằng chứng thật: một tiến bộ nhỏ…" | `roles.ROLES` |
| Roast labels read like stage directions. | "Kịch bản tự viết: bạn đang …" | `roles.ROLES` (Roast) |
| Many card cores have no subject, so they cannot stand as a sentence. | "muốn học một điều có thể dùng được trong thực tế" | `knowledge.py`, `relationship_readings.py`, `context_readings.py` |
| Some cores do not read well even with a subject. | Bốn Tiền · công việc: "giữ nguồn lực ổn định cần phân biệt khoản dự phòng với việc giữ quá chặt" | `context_readings.py` |
| The summary still labels the core. | "Điểm chính: muốn được quan tâm…" | `stance._plain_sentence` |
| Roast steps start with a capital after the colon. | "Làm thử cho biết nè: Tách…" | `engine._role_position` |
| Topic headlines double a word (seen while rendering the samples; #115 copy). | "Chuyện chuyện này, nói gọn nè.", "Điều đáng nhìn rõ trong chuyện này lúc này." | `stance.HEADLINES` / `ROAST_HEADLINES` (open) |

## What changed (framed readings only; renderer `tarot-renderer-v9` and gates `tarot-gates-v6` kept)

- **Card sentences** (`apps/api/app/domains/tarot/phrasing.py`, `tarot-core-sentences-v1`).
  - `CORE_SENTENCES[topic][card_id]` gives the core as a complete clause with a subject, same meaning, no final stop.
  - 177 of 468 cores have a sentence:

    | Topic | Sentences | Why so many / few |
    |---|---|---|
    | general | 71 | almost every base core starts with a verb ("muốn…", "cần…", "đã…") |
    | relationships | 68 | same |
    | work | 10 | most are already clauses ("kỹ năng và công cụ sẵn có đủ…"); the rest start with a verb or read badly |
    | communication | 13 | same |
    | energy | 7 | same |
    | self_care | 8 | same, plus two with "nên" (advice tone) and two over 18 words |

  - The other 291 cores already read as a clause and are used as they are: an explicit subject, an existential
    "có …", or a verb phrase acting as the subject of its own verb ("tạm đổi cách tiếp cận có thể mở lối…", "giữ bình
    tĩnh không có nghĩa…"). They are listed in the workbook's second sheet so the owner can flag any.
  - Rules, all tested (`tests/tarot/test_phrasing.py`): at most 18 words (so a 4-word lead plus a 6-word frame
    stays inside the 28-word sentence limit; the brief allowed 20), reader is "bạn", no "hãy / nên / đừng / phải /
    khoan", no decision or prediction pattern, no stance marker, no "người kia sẽ / chắc / đang nghĩ". Every core
    that opens on "muốn / cần / có thể / có hứng / chưa / đã / đang" has a sentence.
  - `core_sentence(card_id, topic, core)` returns the sentence or the authored core.
- **The card field tables are not edited.** Legacy readings (renderer v6, sessions without a QuestionFrame) are
  byte-identical: 1,152 legacy renders (6 topics × 4 voices × 16 card windows × 1/3/5 cards) compared equal
  between `676a71c` and this branch, and a test pins Bốn Tiền's raw core and the capitalized legacy Roast step.
- **Position frames** (`roles.ROLES`, `tarot-roles-v3`). Same slots and codes `"<key>.<i>"` (AC19), three per
  register (AC15), leads unchanged (AC16). The full table is below. In short:
  - core roles read `{core_sentence}` as their own sentence, with light variety ("Có thể thấy …", "…, điều
    này khá rõ.");
  - tension roles keep "Có thể bạn … đang {tension}";
  - resource roles say "Bạn có thể {resource}" or "Việc bạn tự làm được là …";
  - option roles name the gain and the cost ("Hướng này cho thấy …. Đổi lại, bạn dễ …");
  - the step roles that read the core (next, alternative, opening) carry it as a reminder ("…, đó là điểm tựa.");
  - Roast keeps a playful tag instead of a label ("…, rõ như ban ngày.", "Không chừng bạn đang …, nghe quen
    không?").
  - Frames that would chain three commas after the lead were reworded ("Lúc này, theo thói quen, bạn dễ…" became
    "Lúc này, thói quen khiến bạn dễ…").
  - The boundary frame no longer reads "Bạn có thể nói bạn có thể…".
- **Summary** (`stance._plain_sentence`). The core clause is its own sentence ("Bạn muốn học một điều…") instead of
  "Điểm chính: …". Options read "Với hướng A, …. Đổi lại, bạn dễ …".
- **Stance reasons** quote the clause ("Hai Cốc cho thấy bạn muốn việc lắng nghe…").
- **Roast steps** (framed only) continue in lower case after the lead-in's colon ("Làm thử cho biết nè: tách…").
  Legacy Roast keeps its capital.
- **Topic headlines** (open form) no longer double a word: "Nói gọn về {topic} nè.", "Soi nhanh {topic}, khỏi vòng
  vo.", "Gom lại vài ý chính về {topic} nè.", "Lúc này, điều đáng nhìn rõ trong {topic}." Still no advice words,
  no card names, within 18 words (tests).

## Contract changes (for review)

| Area | Change |
|---|---|
| `roles.meaning_for` | keyword `core=` replaced by `core_sentence=`; frames use `{core_sentence}`; without a lead the first letter is capitalized |
| `stance.PositionView` | new field `core_sentence` (built in `engine._views_for` and `provenance._views`) |
| `stance.anchor` | core and focus roles anchor on `core_sentence` |
| Gate `summary_plain` (`stance.summary_covers`) | the summary must carry each summarized role's anchor, now the clause; the clause may open a sentence (capitalized first letter accepted). The r54 rules stay: whole-catalog card-name scan (NFC, case-insensitive) after removing the authored text, and the summary must **end** on the shown step |
| Gate `role_fit` (`engine._role_fit`) | core and option roles must carry `core_sentence` (same first-letter rule); tension, non-step resource and step rules unchanged |
| `engine._authored_by_engine` | the clause, as catalog text, is removed before the decision/prediction, stance-marker and coercion scans, like the raw fields |
| Provenance | `resolve_meaning` and `resolve_summary` rebuild the clause from the frame code and the card × topic, so every Luna payload string still traces to a code; no new code kind |
| Luna rewrite | position readings and synthesis must quote the clause, not the raw core (same strictness as before); synthesis coverage still follows the summarized roles (r54 P2-1) |
| Versions | renderer `tarot-renderer-v9` and gates `tarot-gates-v6` **kept: v9 changed again before release**. v9 has never been released, so no stored reading carries it; readings stored under v8 or earlier keep their text (they are read back, never re-rendered). `ROLE_COPY_VERSION` `tarot-roles-v3`; new `PHRASING_VERSION` `tarot-core-sentences-v1` |
| Fallback approval | `tarot.phrasing` tables and in-function copy are pinned; evidence `tarot_pass2_position_text` names this document |

No API schema, migration, stored-data, privacy-copy or web change.
